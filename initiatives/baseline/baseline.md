# baseline — mocap-blender

**Goal:** P5 (baseline): view and correct the animation in Blender.

**Scope:** Add-on that imports the `MocapTake` as animated empties (direct visual check of the data) and a bridge to the FreeMoCap add-on to get a quick rig.

**Out of scope:** Custom retargeting to character rigs, facial profile, database link (phase 2).

**Initiative gate:** A real take opens in Blender with animated empties and a base track in the NLA.
